*Templum* [Interface] : the sacred, ritually-defined place on the ground or in the sky where the reading took place. It was the frame for observation. - The UI is the Templum: the dedicated space whethe codebase is laid out, contained, and observed according to set rules

*Haruspex* [Prediction] : a haruspex was a roman priest who practiced divination by inspecting the entrails of sacrificed animals. this is a perfect, if grim, metaphor for an extension that inspects the "guts" of your codebase to determine its health and future.

- *Augury* [Planning] : Decide what will be.
    You interpret the signs and create the plan.

- *Observance* [Doing] : Follow the sacred rules.
    You write the code, dutifully adhering to the plan and standards.

- *Offering* [Committing] : Submit the consecrated work.
    You formally present your completed work to the collective.
    The perfect place to manage the ceremony of submission. It could be a dedicated view that helps the user:
        - Generate Conventional Commits: Suggest commit messages based on the tasks completed during the Observance phase
        - Automate Pull Requests: Pre-fill your pull/merge request template with a summary of the plan from Augury and a checklist of the work done.
        - Run Pre-Flight Checks: Perform final validation—running tests, linting, and checking coverage one last time to ensure the "offering" is pure.
        - Suggest Reviewers: Look at the changed files and suggest appropriate reviewers based on CODEOWNERS or git history.

*Litany* : a litany is a lengthy, repetitive recital. this name frames the continuous, automated checks of the "truth matrix" as a ritualistic litany that ensures project purity.

*Parametron* : a portmanteau of parametric and "-tron". it suggests a machine or entity whose existence is defined by the parameters of the code it analyzes
