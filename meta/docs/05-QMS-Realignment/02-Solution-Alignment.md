## Narrative Summary

- Templum already operates the core skin ingestion and session management paths; the remaining work focuses on adapter stability, storage integration, and validation command exposure.
- Phoenix Code Lite carries the primary backlog for retiring Word and Excel, including the skin exporter, the traceability model, and the release bundler. These items must land before the MVP cutover.
- The Validation System is the critical dependency across traceability and evidence packaging. Deterministic result exports and run metadata must replace the existing Phase 6 approximations.
- Haruspex enhancements are deferred until the backend refactor completes. Once deterministic analysis payloads are available, they will augment logging but they do not block the MVP scope.
- Regulatory clarifications (IEC 62304 class confirmation and any added cloud obligations) may tighten artefact schemas but do not require architectural changes to the alignment described here.
- Design-review capture and the live risk register expand the MVP to cover IEC 62304 Sections 5 and 7 directly, ensuring that approvals and mitigations move out of Word/Excel without weakening compliance evidence.
